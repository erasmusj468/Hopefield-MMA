PRIVACY POLICY.

How Hopefield MMA handles personal information on this website.

Last updated: 24 November 2026

This notice explains how Hopefield MMA handles personal information when you visit this website or contact the club. The club owner should review it before treating it as final legal advice.

1. Who is responsible?

Public-facing name: Hopefield MMA Club (Hopefield MMA).
Location: Hopefield, Western Cape, South Africa.
Phone: 082 867 2395.

Sports affiliation: The club states that it is registered with MMA-SA.

The CIPC certificate has not yet been supplied for this website update. The registered legal name, CIPC registration number and registered address must be added after the owner confirms the certificate. Until then, this website uses the public-facing name Hopefield MMA Club.

2. Information handled

  • Contact enquiries: your name, phone number or email address, and message.
  • Website reviews: your display name, star rating, review text and email address. The email address is kept private and used by the club to contact you about review authenticity. A one-way hash of the network address may be stored for basic spam prevention.
  • Information you choose to share when calling, emailing or contacting the club through WhatsApp.
  • Public club content: athlete names, profiles, competition records and photographs.
  • Technical request information that the hosting provider may process to deliver and secure the website.

Please do not send identity numbers, payment-card details, medical records or other sensitive information through the website contact form.

3. Contact form

The contact form prepares an email using your device's default email application. The website does not submit the form to a website database or store the form message on its own server. Your email application opens a draft; the enquiry is sent only if you choose to send it. Your email provider then handles the message.

4. Website ratings and reviews

When you submit a review, the display name, rating and review text are saved in the club website database and published automatically. The email address you provide is not shown publicly and is collected so the club can contact you to check whether the review is genuine. A one-way hash of the network address may be used to limit spam submissions. Reviews may be removed if they are found to be fake, abusive, unlawful or otherwise unsuitable. Do not include sensitive personal information in a review. If you are under 18, a parent or guardian must agree to the submission and publication.

Review data is hosted by Neon as the database provider and the website is hosted by Vercel. These providers may process data outside South Africa. Contact the club if you want to ask about or request correction or deletion of your review information, subject to applicable law.

5. Purposes

Information is used where reasonably needed to respond to enquiries, communicate about training or membership, manage club administration, maintain website security or meet legal obligations. The club should not request more information than is needed for the purpose.

6. Service providers and external links

  • Vercel: hosts the website and may process technical information needed to deliver and secure it.
  • Neon: hosts the database used to store and retrieve website reviews.
  • Google Fonts: the site requests font files from Google's service when a page loads.
  • WhatsApp and Instagram: links may take you to those services; their own privacy terms apply if you use them.

The current website code does not include an analytics tracker, advertising pixel, embedded social feed or embedded video player. Some external providers may process technical data outside South Africa depending on their infrastructure.

7. Sharing and retention

The club does not sell contact enquiries. Information may be shared with service providers where needed to operate communications or the website, or where disclosure is required by law. Communications should be kept only as long as reasonably necessary, subject to applicable record-keeping obligations.

8. Your rights

Subject to the conditions and limits in the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA), you may ask whether the club holds personal information about you, request access, ask for incorrect information to be corrected, or request deletion where the law allows. You may also object to certain processing or withdraw consent where processing is based on consent. Contact the club using the details above.

You may also contact the Information Regulator of South Africa or email POPIAComplaints@inforegulator.org.za.

9. Children and photographs

The club obtains consent before publishing photographs of its athletes. Where an athlete is under 18, the club should also ensure that any additional parent or guardian permission required by law is in place. Athletes or parents/guardians may contact the club to request a review of a photograph or profile. The club should keep appropriate media-permission records.

10. Security and updates

The club should take reasonable technical and organisational steps to protect personal information. No website or email service can guarantee absolute security. This notice may be updated when the website, club processes or legal details change.

Owner action: confirm the CIPC legal name and registered address, identify the person responsible for privacy requests, and ensure athlete/guardian photo permissions are in place before treating this notice as final.
