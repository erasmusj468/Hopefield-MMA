THE CLUB

More than training.
It's competition.

Hopefield MMA is a combat-sports club based in Hopefield, Western Cape. The club provides a place to train, develop skills, build discipline and, for those who choose to compete, test those skills in competition.

The club's fighters have represented Hopefield MMA at events including the 2025 SA MMA Nationals.
