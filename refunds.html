REFUND POLICY.

How to ask about cancellations and refunds for Hopefield MMA membership and sessions.

Last updated: 24 November 2026

This is an interim website refund and cancellation notice. The club owner must confirm the club's actual membership and session arrangements before this page is treated as the final refund policy.

1. Website payments

The website does not currently accept online payments or bookings. Prices on the Training page are informational. Confirm the current fee, what it covers and the cancellation terms with the club before paying.

2. Refund and cancellation requests

Contact the club as soon as possible by calling 082 867 2395 or emailing erasmusj468@gmail.com. Include the service or membership concerned, payment date, amount and a short explanation. The club will review the request against any written terms agreed at registration and the law that applies.

3. Consumer rights

Nothing in this notice removes rights or remedies provided by applicable South African law, including the Consumer Protection Act 68 of 2008 and, where a transaction qualifies, the Electronic Communications and Transactions Act 25 of 2002. Which cancellation or refund rights apply depends on the agreement and how it was concluded.

4. Terms the owner must confirm

The owner should set out in writing how the club handles monthly membership cancellations, missed sessions, private-session cancellations, group-session cancellations, club-cancelled sessions and payments made in advance. Any terms must be fair and consistent with applicable law.

Important: No blanket “no refunds” rule has been added. The owner has not supplied the club's exact refund terms, so this page does not invent them or limit statutory rights.
